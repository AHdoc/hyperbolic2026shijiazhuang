Venue

Location & Transport

Join us at Hebei Normal University in the heart of Shijiazhuang.

🏫 Conference Venue

Hebei Normal University
Shijiazhuang, Hebei Province
People's Republic of China

The conference will be held on the main campus of Hebei Normal University, providing a vibrant academic setting for five days of mathematical exchange.

✈ By Air

Shijiazhuang Zhengding International Airport (SJW) serves domestic and international flights. The airport is approximately 40 km from the city center.

🚈 By High-Speed Rail

Shijiazhuang Railway Station is well-connected to major Chinese cities via high-speed rail (Beijing: ~1.5h, Shanghai: ~4h, Xi'an: ~3h).
