🛣 Travel & Accommodation

✈ Option 1: Fly to Shijiazhuang

The most direct route is to fly to Shijiazhuang Zhengding International Airport (石家庄正定国际机场, SJW). Note that international flights may require a transfer to a domestic flight during your trip.

✈ Option 2: Fly to Beijing, then take a train

There are two international airports in Beijing. From either airport, you can take a high-speed train to Shijiazhuang.

Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) · 北京首都国际机场
Airport shuttle bus to Beijing West Railway Station (北京西站): ~1h 20min (traffic dependent). Many trains to Shijiazhuang daily. Last high-speed train from Beijing West Railway Station to Shijiazhuang departs at 22:04. Journey time: 1h – 1h 40min.
Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX) · 北京大兴国际机场
One direct train to Shijiazhuang per day: departs 20:03, arrives 22:44. Alternatively, go to Beijing West Railway Station first by subway (~1h) or train (~20–35min), then take a regular high-speed train.

Note: Please arrive at the train station gate at least 5 minutes before departure.

🚈 Option 3: Already in China

If you are already visiting China, you may take a domestic flight or train directly to Shijiazhuang.

🏢 Hotel: Guanhe International Hotel

We recommend the Guanhe International Hotel (观和国际酒店), conveniently located right at the east gate of the university.
